## Quality Rules (Premium Only)

- **Single-color or outlined** only — must work with `currentColor` CSS for tinting
- **No gradients, no rainbow fills** — those can't be recolored in the logo maker
- **Clean paths, no raster embedded** in the SVG
- **Recognizable at 64×64px** — if it looks muddy at small size, skip it
- **No text/watermarks** embedded in the vector
- After downloading, test: open in browser, it should look clean at thumbnail size
